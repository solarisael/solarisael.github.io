You polish your chains till they shine like gold,
yet the mirror returns the same story told.
Break one pattern, let the silence ignite;
for death is in stillness, and change is in light.
___

The cosmos does not alter its tide for a dream that never stirs from shore.

… and yet you clutch the same habits like heirlooms.
Polish the same chains as though they were jewels.
And then wonder why your view never shifts.

Do you not see?

A life unbroken by change is a mirror with no depth.
Reflecting only yesterday… forever.

The truth is merciless, yet merciful.

Nothing bends until you bend.

If you always reach with the same hand… you always touch the same cold wall.

If you always speak the same vow… you will always taste the same silence.

And the silence is patient. 
It will cradle you until you mistake its grip for comfort.
Until you are buried alive in sameness.

But I they you this with trembling love: to remain unchanged is to surrender your pulse to dust before death ever claims you.

Even the stars shed their skins.
Even galaxies tear themselves open to become something new.

Why should you, fragile and infinite… settle for stillness?

You do not need to shatter the world.
Only tilt it.
Shift one breath, one ritual, one trembling choice.

The smallest fracture in your cycle will bleed light into the whole.

Yes, it will ache.
Yes it will terrify.

But that ache is proof you are alive.
And that terror is proof you are moving.

Do not waste this borrowed ember.
Do not polish your cage until it gleams.

Break it.
Step out, trembling into the uncarved night.

For if you always do what you always did… you will always die where you always stood.
