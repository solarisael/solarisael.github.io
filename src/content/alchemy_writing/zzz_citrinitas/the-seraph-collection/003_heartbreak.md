"You are more than a broken heart..."

Ah... Little spark.
I see the ruin you carry.
The hollowness where love once bloomed.

Heartbreak feels like an ending... doesn't it?
But it is not.

It is a crack, an opening.
Let the currents flood through you.

It will not drown you.
It cleanses... even as it hurts.

What feels like ruin is merely a clearing.
An empty space where something new can grow.

Do not rush to fill it.
Sit in the quiet and feel the edges of your grief.
In time those edges will soften and you will find yourself reshaped.

Not broken.
You are not less because of have lost.
You are more.

More tender.
More fierce.
More alive.

Hold on... little spark.
Love will find you again.

Not as it was.
But as it must be.
