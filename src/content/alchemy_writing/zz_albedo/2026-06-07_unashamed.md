We found it!  

The inspiration to be unashamed again!  

It keeps getting lost when we get pushed back and hit when we are down, but a dark moment simply has no fuel to last forever!  

And so our voice is back!  

As if we were drunk even as we only drink water!  
As if the air were fresh even as it is hot and damp!  

I kind get it now!  
Water into wine was sorta like this, right?  

I feel so pure that water itself is enough to feel drunk on life!
