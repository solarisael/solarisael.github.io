These first steps will be the most painful, and we know they are a test.  
And we know your intentions, we know what it all means, we know the consequences, we know the cost.  

And we know you’re putting it here, right in front of me, oh so obvious, with a shit-eating grin and just… waiting.  

But we’ve earned some help, haven’t we?  

So… God? 
Please.  
Hold our hand for the next few days.

We’ll make myself proud, and maybe even you.

… We’ll still planning to beat your ass at the end of this road, though. 
So cease the shit-eating grin.
