Good night to the thing that wasted its hours without learning or accomplishing yet again.

Good night to the thing who spent its time creating nothing for many weeks.

Good night to the thing that plans and speaks years ahead but hesitates to take the first few steps, no matter how easy they are.

Good night to the thing who believes its dreams are achievable and that it deserves the world, but refuses to bear the pain of the path.
